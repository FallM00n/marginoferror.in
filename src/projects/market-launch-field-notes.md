A proposition that works in one market rarely survives contact with the next one unchanged. These are ongoing field notes on what actually bends: the pitch, the format, the price, the channel, or the assumption underneath all of them.

## The questions, before execution hardens them

The framework is mostly a set of questions asked early, while the plan is still soft: where does attention actually live here, what does the audience already pay for, and which habit are we asking them to break.

This one stays open on purpose. It is a way of looking, not a finished answer.
